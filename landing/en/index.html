Study and organize your life with files you own

NoteVault is a desktop app with Markdown notes, a Mentor that builds your study plan, and tasks, habits, goals and finances. No account, no server, no telemetry.

Download Read the docs

Windows installer. Linux and macOS from source.

Everything in one place

What you usually spread across several apps, stored in one folder on your disk.

Markdown notes

Live preview, links between notes, formulas and canvases to map out ideas. They are plain .md files.

Study Mentor

A plan that fits your dates, with spaced reviews, Pomodoro and the daily pace you need in each subject.

Theory, map and practice

Each topic becomes a note, a concept map and a practice file with exercises.

Personal management

Tasks, projects, calendar, habits, goals, finances, exercise and nutrition.

Optional AI

Use your own API, a local model (Ollama, LM Studio) or a prompt you paste yourself. It works without AI too.

Editable skills

The instructions that generate your notes are in plain view, with version history and a warning for anything left outdated.

Your data, your folder

The vault is a folder on your disk with text any editor can open.

No account, no cloud

There is no NoteVault server. Nothing leaves your computer except what you send to the AI provider you choose.

Open format

Markdown with frontmatter and JSON, documented and versioned. Compatible with Obsidian.

Sync your way

Syncthing, Git or your favorite cloud on the same folder. A backup is just copying the folder.

Free, and kept alive by your support

The app is free and open source. We are students with no funding or financial backing: any help, however small, lets us keep developing NoteVault.

Frequently asked questions

Who is behind NoteVault?

Lessteps, a team of students who build software. “NoteVault” is a provisional name for the product; the company behind it is Lessteps. If the name changes, we will announce it here and in the repository.

Do I need to pay for AI to use it?

No. NoteVault works without AI. If you want it, you can use a free local model or the API of your choice.

Where is my data stored?

In the folder you choose, as Markdown and JSON files. NoteVault has no servers and sends no telemetry.

Is there a mobile or web version?

Not yet. They are planned; the vault format is already designed so a mobile client can read and write the same files.

If it is open source, what is sold?

The code is free. What is sold is ready-made study content and, voluntarily, support for the project.

Can I modify it or use it at my company?

Yes, under the terms of the GPL-3.0. If you distribute a modified version, you must publish its source code. The name and logo have a separate policy.
